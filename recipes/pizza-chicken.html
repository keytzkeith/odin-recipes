Pizza Chicken


Credit: Sara Haas

Description

This 30-minute recipe combines two fan-favorites: easy chicken casseroles and pizza!

If you like pizza, then you’ll love One-Pan Pizza Chicken. It’s as simple as building a pizza (just the toppings) on top of chicken. First, chicken breasts are pounded to 1/4-inch thickness to help them cook quickly and evenly. Next, the chicken is topped with tomatoes, balsamic vinegar, seasonings, and cheese. Then, it’s all about the toppings, which include (but definitely aren’t limited to) pepperoni, black olives, and green bell pepper. You build it all in the pan you’re baking it in which makes cooking and cleaning simple. Bake at 425 for about 20 minutes and you’re ready to eat!


Ingredients

  • 1 (15-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, divided
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder, divided
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder, divided
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 1/2 pounds chicken breast,pounded to 1/4-inch thickness
  • 1/8 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
  • 3/4 cup shredded Italian blend cheese
  • 1/4 cup sliced pepperoni
  • 1 (2.25 ounce) can sliced black olives
  • 1/4 cup diced green bell pepper

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F (220 degrees C).
  2. Lay chicken on top of tomatoes and season with the remaining 1/4 teaspoon salt and pepper.
  3. Cover chicken with remaining crushed tomatoes then drizzle with vinegar. Sprinkle remaining 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/2 teaspoon onion powder, and 1/2 teaspoon Italian seasoning over everything, then cover with cheese.
  4. Top with pepperoni, black olives, and peppers.
  5. Bake until cheese is bubbling and melted and chicken has reached an internal temperature of at least 165 degrees F (74 degrees C), about 20 minutes.
